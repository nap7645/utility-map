# CA interconnection gaps (2026-09-23)

## Answered (Primary)
- **Adding a battery to NEM 2.0:** keeps the 20-year legacy period. PG&E NEM2 SC 8.b (Sheet 32) and SCE NEM-ST SC 8.d (Sheet 33) cite D.22-12-056. **Exception** under D.24-03-071: an SGIP-funded battery with a reservation accepted on or after 2024-06-04 moves the customer to NBT. The equity, low-income resiliency and San Joaquin Valley residential budgets are exempt. Legacy NEM-paired storage can later grow by at most 10% (SCE NEM-ST SC 6.e). NBT customers who add only storage keep their 9-year NBT legacy period (SCE NBT SC 7.b).
- **Grid charging (NBT and NEM 2.0):** Yes-but-not-credited. A Directly Connected storage device "may also be charged from the grid" (SCE NBT SC 1.g). Exports are credited only for renewable-derived energy:
  - Storage of 10 kW or less: a monthly PV-estimate cap applies.
  - Storage above 10 kW: needs added metering or certified power control (no storage export, or no grid charging).
