# chunk_TX_tdu gaps (ERCOT TDUs + ERCOT statewide), last run 2026-09-27

Scope: 5 ERCOT TDUs + "ERCOT (statewide)". REP offers (TOU, free nights, REP VPP plans) were out of scope and not researched.

## Could not verify / stale sources
- **CenterPoint Energy Houston Electric**: no 2026 load management manuals found. The CLM/WLM rows use the 2025 / 2025-26 manual ($40/kW), and the Residential LM row uses the 2025 guidelines ($35/kW, 1.2 kW/ESIID cap). A guessed 2026 URL on visionelements.programprocessing.com returned nothing. The CNP program pages are JS-only and tell you to email CNPEE@CenterPointEnergy.com for manuals. The 2026 EEPR (PUCT Project 59166) exists, but the interchange pages would not render, so 2026 rates and budgets are unconfirmed.
- **CenterPoint Residential Solar Program**: the $135/kW-DC, 15 kW cap figure comes only from an installer site (Secondary). The $1M 2025 budget comes from EnergyChoiceMatters' report on the EEPR. I found no CNP primary page, and it is unknown whether storage gets an adder.
- **CenterPoint DG export row**: the text ("CenterPoint does not buy power or offer net metering"; the REP sets buyback) comes from centerpointenergy.com search snippets. The DGAP-FAQ and residential-solar pages are JS-only and came back blank from the fetch. The row is marked Primary, but a browser check is advisable.
- **CenterPoint REP Smart Thermostat Program**: $100 per ENERGY STAR thermostat paid to REPs, $2M 2025 budget (EnergyChoiceMatters). No row was written because it is an EE device incentive, not DR. The 2026 market-call PDF on centerpointenergy.com returned blank.
- **TNMP Residential Load Management Program**: the tnmp.com/residentiallm page confirms the program exists but publishes no incentive, event rules or sponsor terms. The row has blank values. TNMP's REP Smart Thermostat pilot (listed at $100k in a 2025 report) was not verified.
- **Oncor residential/commercial solar**: there is no published $/W rate. Incentives come from the EEPM calculator (TRM/PVWatts) and are capped at 50% of project cost. The $9,000-per-home maximum seen on third-party sites was not verified and was not used.
- **Oncor Winter programs**: only the 2025-26 winter manuals were found. The 2026-27 winter program (normally opens Oct 1) was not yet posted/found.
- **AEP Texas Central / North**: the LM manual is 2025-2026. The web page says 500 kW peak demand minimum, but the manual says a 10 kW minimum per site (summer). Both are recorded in the row. The AEP Texas Residential BYOD page says $15 "per device" and the FAQ says "per household". The txreincentives.com home page still shows "Program Year 2024". The 2026 values in the rows come from the 2026 SMART Source kickoff deck on aeptxsaves.com.
- **ERCOT ADER**: I could not parse the Phase 3.3 Governing Document (.docx). Limits come from market notice M-A030226-01 (500 MW registered / 100 MW AS / 90% QSE, effective 2026-03-02). The Oct-2025 participation figures and the Aggregate NCLR model details come from the ERCOT WMS presentation (2025-11-05) and the Board item 4.3 (June 2025). The ADER row cites only the market notice URL. Current 2026 participation MW (ADER Monthly Report xlsx) were not pulled.
- **ADER customer payments**: Tesla Electric's support page says compensation is a monthly per-Powerwall VPP credit plus sellback credits, but gives no current $ figure. The older "$10/Powerwall/month" appears only in search snippets and was not used. **Base Power**'s compensation model was not researched (it is a REP, so out of scope).
- **ERS**: no clearing prices recorded. They are in the ERCOT "ERS Procurement Results" (NP3-144-M) data product, which I did not download. Battery-specific ERS qualification rules (the ERS Technical Requirements .docx) were not parsed, so storage_eligible is set to Yes-restricted.
- **Texas Backup Power Package**: the $500/kW grant cap comes from TXSES's summary of the proposed rule (Secondary). The final adopted rule text (PUCT Project 59024, adopted 2026-05-07) could not be fetched from interchange. I could not confirm whether the final rule changed the grant level.
- **NPRR 1296 (ERCOT Residential DR program)**: still Pending/tabled per the ERCOT issue page, with the latest comments dated 2026-07-21. No adopted payment rate.

## Looked for and found nothing
- **PUCT/TEF storage or resiliency incentive for ordinary residential or commercial customers**: none found. TBPP covers critical facilities only, and TEF's other programs are generation loans/completion bonuses.
- **TDU-run EV managed-charging or EV rate programs** (Oncor, CenterPoint, AEP Texas, TNMP): none found. The only EV items are the EVSE add-ons in AEP Texas SMART Source ($500 with solar) and TNMP Res/HTR SOP ($500 with solar).
- **TDU battery DR / storage dispatch programs**: none found as stand-alone programs. Batteries can only enter the TDU load management SOPs as curtailable load. CenterPoint's CLM manual excludes power exported to the grid from "curtailable load", so storage eligibility in all LM rows is Unclear.
- **4CP transmission-charge avoidance** (Demand-Charge row per TDU suggested in plans/rto_ercot.md): not written this round. It needs a TDU tariff citation per company.
