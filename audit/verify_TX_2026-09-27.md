## Check 2 — Blank areas in Nevada and Idaho (Utility color mode)

### Nevada — northern NV / NV-ID border blank patch

Visually, a whitish blob appears roughly over lat 41–42.1°N, lng -114.4 to -116°W (north-central Nevada near the Idaho border, north of the "Nevada" label / east of Twin Falls). Pixel-level canvas sampling (reading the actual `<canvas>` alpha channel, not just the screenshot) showed this area is a **mix of true gaps (alpha = 0, ~35% of sampled pixels) and correctly-colored polygon edges** (Idaho Power, PacifiCorp, Bonneville Power Administration, Raft River Rural Electric Coop, Wells Rural Electric, WAPA) whose pale, semi-transparent fills (`fillOpacity: 0.5`) make the composite look washed-out/white in a normal screenshot even where something is drawn.

Sampled 5 confirmed-transparent (alpha=0) points and queried the HIFLD service directly:
- (42.92, -116.43), (41.62, -116.32), (41.46, -116.10), (41.38, -115.88), (40.96, -115.66) → **all returned zero HIFLD features.**

**Classification: (1) genuinely unserved.** This is sparsely populated high-desert/BLM land on the NV/ID border; HIFLD itself has no retail-territory polygon there, so the app isn't dropping real coverage — there's nothing to draw. Not a defect in the app's data pipeline.

*Secondary finding while investigating this area (not a blank-area issue, but worth flagging as a data-model inconsistency):* Idaho Power (EIA 9191) has full presence data in `PRES` (four "Yes" offerings, all Primary-sourced) and does render with an assigned fill color, but `DATA.utilities[9191]` is `undefined` — i.e. it's missing from whatever `DATA.utilities` is used for (search/legend counting/etc.). Since it's a WECC-nonRTO IOU with real program data, this looks like an omission in the `DATA.utilities` list-building step, not something end-users would see as a visual bug, but should be checked against whatever `DATA.utilities` feeds (e.g. utility search/autocomplete would silently exclude Idaho Power).

### Idaho — central-Idaho north–south corridor

A much larger and more clearly blank vertical stripe runs through central Idaho (roughly the Frank Church–River of No Return / Selway-Bitterroot wilderness / Payette-Salmon National Forest corridor, lat ~44.7–48°N, lng ~-114.7 to -116°W), visible as a solid white band on both sides of the "Idaho" map label, running north from around Boise's latitude up past Missoula's longitude band.

Canvas sampling confirmed genuine alpha=0 gaps (259 transparent samples out of a dense grid over that stripe). Queried HIFLD at 6 confirmed-blank points:
- (47.89, -115.99), (46.82, -115.64), (47.06, -115.29), (45.97, -115.11), (45.48, -114.94), (44.73, -114.76) → **all returned zero HIFLD features.**

**Classification: (1) genuinely unserved.** This is essentially uninhabited national-forest/wilderness terrain with no retail electric customers, consistent with zero HIFLD territory coverage. Not a defect.

No evidence of classification (2) — a HIFLD territory that exists but the app fails to load/filed under the wrong state — was found in either state at the sampled points; all blank points independently returned empty from HIFLD too.

## Defects list (with exact fixes)

1. **Sentinel leak in customer count.** Oncor (44372) and CenterPoint (8901) show `"-999,999 customers"` in the drawer header instead of an empty/unknown-count treatment. `presence.csv` has these fields blank. Fix: wherever `CUSTOMERS`/`customers` is formatted for display, treat `-999999` (and blank) as null → print nothing or "customer count not available".

2. **Retail-choice explanation missing from the UI.** `presence.csv`'s `scan_notes` for Oncor/CenterPoint explicitly says the "None found" for time-based pricing is because ERCOT retail choice puts TOU rates on the REP, not the TDU — but this text is never rendered in the drawer (no inline note, no tooltip/title attribute on the "None found" cell). Fix: surface `scan_notes` (or a new dedicated field) as a tooltip/inline caption on the "Time-based pricing: None found" row for TDUs in retail-choice markets.

3. **Minor RTO-bucket gap.** Three Texas co-ops/munis (16821 Sam Rayburn Municipal Power Agency, 17583 South Texas Electric Coop, 17561 South Plains Electric Coop) fall through `rtoOf()` into `OTHER` or the non-standard string `"ERCOT/SPP split"`, which isn't one of the enumerated legend categories. Fix: give these three an explicit RTO/ISO classification in `rtoOf()` rather than defaulting to `OTHER`, and standardize `"ERCOT/SPP split"` to a real legend value (or split the co-op's territory features so each part gets a single RTO tag).

4. **`DATA.utilities` omission (low severity, found incidentally).** Idaho Power (EIA 9191) has full `PRES` presence data and renders correctly on the map, but is absent from `DATA.utilities`. Not visually apparent, but any feature keyed off `DATA.utilities` (search, listings, etc.) would silently skip Idaho Power. Fix: audit `DATA.utilities` construction to ensure every ID present in `PRES` is also present there.

No defects found in the NV/ID blank-area check — both sampled blank regions are genuinely unserved federal/wilderness land with zero corresponding HIFLD territory, not app data-loading failures.
